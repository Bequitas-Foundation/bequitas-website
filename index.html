Our Story

Education is the root. Everything else grows from it.

Bequitas Foundation Limited was founded on a simple belief: talent and potential are distributed equally across Hong Kong, but opportunity is not. We work to close that gap.

We partner with schools, community organizations, and families to provide the resources, mentorship, and support that underprivileged students and communities need to thrive — not as charity given from a distance, but as a hand offered alongside.

Having recently received Section 88 tax-exempt status, we're building the foundation for long-term, sustainable impact across Hong Kong.

What We Do

Two commitments, one purpose

Every program we run traces back to the same goal: giving people the footing to build a better future for themselves.

🎓

Advancement of Education

Scholarships, tutoring, learning resources, and mentorship for students who show promise but lack the means to pursue it — from primary school through tertiary education.

🤝

Underprivileged Community Support

Direct support for low-income families and communities across Hong Kong, including essentials, community programs, and partnerships with local organizations already doing this work on the ground.
